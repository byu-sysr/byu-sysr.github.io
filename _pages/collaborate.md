Our group covers a wide range of topics in systems, security and privacy;
we are always open to exploring collaboration opportunities (whether
academic, industry or other groups). If you are interested in collaborating or otherwise want to connect to us,
[contact us here](/contact).

### Masters/Ph.D. Student Opportunities

Interested grad students should apply to the
BYU Computer Science department.
Information can be found on the
[department website](https://cs.byu.edu/education/graduate/admissions-information/).

### Undergraduate Research

Current undergraduate BYU students who are interested in carrying out
novel research on automated vulnerability discovery techniques,
security analysis and assurance for systems applications, or
cellular and mobile network operation and security are invited to
[reach out](/contact) to Prof. Bennett.
